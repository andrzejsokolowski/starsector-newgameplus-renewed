[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Every level you gain and every great feat, in any save, earns career points.
Every new game opens a store next to your starting fleet, where those points buy a head start.
Needs LunaLib and Achievements - Renewed.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Career points[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Each level you gain pays 1 career point. Each save pays each level once, even if you reload an older save file.[/li]
[li]Career points are kept outside your saves, so deleting saves never touches them.[/li]
[li]They are never spent. Every new game gets a budget equal to the points you earned in your other saves. Points you earn during a game go to your next games.[/li]
[li]Levels bought in the store pay nothing.[/li]
[li]Feats pay once per profile, never again in any save: At the Gates 20, the Planetkiller recovered 20, an Omega fleet beaten 30, the Ziggurat defeated 20, the Galatia Academy story 10, the Usurpers 10, the Knight Errant 10, a duel won 5, Kanta's protection 5.[/li]
[li]Feats from other mods, when installed: Hall of Triumph: Isa's masterwork 10, Gan Eden 30. Secrets of the Frontier: A Memory 15, The Wayward Star 30, A Lost Thread 10, Legacy of Hypnos 10, Annex-Barrow 15, Hope for Hallowhall 10. Random Assortment of Things: the Singularity 30. Knights of Ludd: Tri-Tachyon black site 20, Elysia 20, Dawn 15, Dusk 15. Heggie Tales 15. Sol Renewed: The Deterrent 30, Transferring Sol 5. Nexerelin: Salvation 30. Sephira Conclave: Operation Burning Storm 20. Liminal: the Liminal Crisis 20. What We Left Behind: the IAIIC crisis 30, the Omega Manufactorum 20. Domain Phase Lab: Project Prometheus 20, the Helheim Supremacy 10. Diable Avionics: the First Relay 20. Emergent Threats: A Thousand Cuts 20, Shattered Fortress 15. Halo Dynamics: the Culann mission 15. Core of Babel: Babel Protocol 15. Crab Shack: Dream No More 20. Tahlan Additions: The Elegy of Opis 15, The Illustrious 10. United Aurora Federation: Tilted Dagger 15.[/li]
[li]Every achievement in Achievements - Renewed pays career points once per profile, from any mod. Rule Book - Renewed challenges pay nothing.[/li]
[li]The feats are achievements too, each in the tab of its mod. With Rule Book - Renewed, a feat it also has shows up once.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]The Ledger[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]A station that appears next to your fleet when a game starts, or the first time you load an older save with the mod installed.[/li]
[li]It stays for the whole game and has its own intel entry, so you can always find it on the map.[/li]
[li]The store has tabs and a scrolling list. Click Buy, then Confirm.[/li]
[li]Story points, skill points, levels and credits apply at once. Goods wait in the delivery bay and you take them through the usual loot screen.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]On offer[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Command: story points (3, 10, 30 or 100), skill points (2 per game), levels.[/li]
[li]Logistics: credits, supplies, fuel, crew, marines, heavy machinery, and a perk: +10 percent cargo, fuel and crew capacity for the rest of the game.[/li]
[li]Rare: an Omega core, and with the right mods a Tenebrian Nanoforge (AoTD - Vaults of Knowledge) and an Arc Sustainer (Sephira Conclave), 1 per game each.[/li]
[li]With Peerless Skills: a Peerless point (1 per game).[/li]
[li]Shipyard: S-mod slot (+1), D-mod removal, Stat tuning and Extra ordnance points (2 / 6 / 15 / 40 by hull size). They arrive as cargo items; right-click one to fit it to a ship.[/li]
[li]Stat tuning: hull, armor, flux capacity, flux dissipation, top speed or weapon range +10 percent, peak performance time +20 percent, or supply use -10 percent. Each ship can take each stat once.[/li]
[li]Armoury: one weapon pack per faction, two of every weapon that faction knows, 20 points each. Modded factions get their own packs.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Settings and compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]LunaLib settings: extra career points (set your total by hand), points per level, store price multiplier.[/li]
[li]Works with Nexerelin starts: the Ledger waits until your fleet is placed.[/li]
[li]With Rule Book - Renewed, harder rules earn more career points and easier rules fewer.[/li]
[li]Can be added to a game in progress.[/li]
[/list]
