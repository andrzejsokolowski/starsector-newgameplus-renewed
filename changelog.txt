Version 0.4.0 (2026-11-10)
- Feats now earn career points, once per profile: At the Gates 20, the Planetkiller recovered 20, an Omega fleet beaten 30, the Ziggurat defeated 20, the Galatia Academy story 10, the Usurpers 10, the Knight Errant 10, a duel won 5, Kanta's protection 5.
- A feat already finished in a save you load pays when you load it.

Version 0.3.0 (2026-11-10)
- Weapon packs now go by faction: one pack per faction with every weapon it knows, two of each, 10 points each. Modded factions get their own packs, and blueprints a faction learns during the game are included.
- New in the Rare tab: Tenebrian Nanoforge (with AoTD - Vaults of Knowledge, 10 points) and Arc Sustainer (with Sephira Conclave, 8 points), 1 per game each.
- New in the Command tab: a Peerless point (with Peerless Skills, 10 points, 1 per game).

Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-10)
- New Shipyard tab: S-mod slot (+1), D-mod removal, Stat tuning and Extra ordnance points (2 / 6 / 15 / 40 by hull size). They arrive as cargo items; right-click one to fit it to a ship.
- Stat tuning raises one stat of your choice on one ship: hull, armor, flux capacity, flux dissipation, top speed or weapon range by 10 percent, peak performance time by 20 percent, or cuts supply use by 10 percent. Each ship can take each stat once.
- New perk in the Logistics tab: +10 percent cargo, fuel and crew capacity for the rest of the game (10 points, once per game).
- New Armoury tab: one weapon pack per design type with 5 or more weapons, two of each weapon. Modded design types get their own packs.

Version 0.1.0 (2026-11-09)
- First version.
- Career points: 1 per level gained in any save, kept outside your saves.
- The Ledger: a store next to your starting fleet in every new game, marked on the intel screen.
- Store tabs Command, Logistics and Rare: story points, skill points (2 per game), levels, credits, supplies, fuel, crew, marines, heavy machinery and an Omega core (1 per game).
- Goods are collected through a loot screen and wait in the delivery bay until taken.
- LunaLib settings: extra career points, points per level, store price multiplier.
